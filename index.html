Pending pricing
How a single-price Dutch auction prices this deal.
  1. Investors submit a rate they require and a par amount, in $100,000 increments.
  2. Orders are stacked from the lowest rate up until the $500,000,000 target is filled.
  3. The rate that fills the last bond becomes the clearing rate.
  4. Every winning investor pays that one rate — including those who bid lower.
  5. Bids above the clearing rate get nothing; bids above the reserve never qualify.

Demonstration only. Investor names, order sizes, rates and the offering itself are fabricated for illustration.
